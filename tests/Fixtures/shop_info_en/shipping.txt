Shipping and Delivery

Shipping costs within Germany

Within Germany a flat shipping charge of 4.95 euro applies per order. Orders with a goods value of 75 euro or more ship free of charge within Germany. Bulky items, in particular complete bicycles, carry a surcharge of 29.00 euro.

Shipping costs to other European countries

Delivery to Austria and the Netherlands costs 9.95 euro. For all other European Union countries we charge 14.95 euro. We do not currently offer free shipping outside Germany.

Delivery times

Items held in stock are dispatched the same day when ordered before 2 pm on a working day. Delivery within Germany then normally takes one to three working days. Allow three to five working days for Austria and the Netherlands, and five to eight working days for other EU countries.

If an item is not in stock we state the expected delivery time on the product page. Should a delivery be delayed by more than ten working days we will notify you by email and you may cancel the order free of charge.

Partial deliveries

If you order several items with different availability we will ship in partial consignments on request. No additional shipping costs arise for you.

Carriers and tracking

We ship with DHL and, for bulky items, by freight forwarder. As soon as your consignment leaves our warehouse you will receive an email with the tracking number so you can follow its status online at any time.

Delivery to parcel lockers

Delivery to DHL parcel lockers is possible within Germany provided the consignment is not classed as bulky. Enter your post number during checkout.

Collection in person

Collection from our Hamburg-Billbrook warehouse is possible by prior appointment. No shipping costs apply in that case.
