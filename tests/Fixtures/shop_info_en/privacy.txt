Privacy Policy

Controller

The controller for data processing on this website is Beispiel Sportartikel GmbH, Musterstrasse 12, 20095 Hamburg. You can reach our data protection officer at datenschutz@beispiel-sport.de.

Collection and storage of personal data

When you place an order we collect your first and last name, your delivery and billing address, your email address and the payment details you choose. We process this data to perform the contract of sale on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation.

When you visit our website server log files are stored. These record the page requested, the time of the request, the volume of data transferred, the browser and operating system used, and a truncated IP address. This data is deleted automatically after seven days.

Retention periods

We retain order data for ten years where commercial and tax law retention obligations require it. You may have your customer account deleted at any time; data not subject to a retention obligation is then removed within thirty days.

Disclosure to third parties

To arrange delivery we pass your name and delivery address to the shipping company you select. To process payment we transmit the necessary data to the relevant payment service provider. No further transfer to third parties takes place; in particular we do not sell your data.

Cookies

We use technically necessary cookies required to operate the shopping basket and the login. These require no consent. Analytics cookies are set only with your express consent, which you may withdraw at any time via the Cookie settings link in the page footer.

Your rights

You have the right of access to the data stored about you, the right to rectification of inaccurate data, the right to erasure, the right to restriction of processing and the right to data portability. We answer a request for access within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The competent authority is the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

Objection to marketing

You may object at any time to the use of your email address for sending newsletters, at no cost other than the transmission costs at base rates.
