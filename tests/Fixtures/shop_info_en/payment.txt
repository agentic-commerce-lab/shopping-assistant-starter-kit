Payment Methods

Accepted payment methods

You may pay by advance bank transfer, PayPal, credit card or invoice. We accept Visa and Mastercard. Purchase on invoice is available from your second order onwards and up to a goods value of 500 euro.

When payment is due

For advance bank transfer the amount is payable within seven days of the contract being concluded. For purchase on invoice the amount is due in full within fourteen days of receiving the goods.

Late payment

If you fall into arrears we charge default interest of five percentage points above the base rate. A reminder fee of 2.50 euro applies to the second and each subsequent reminder.

Instalments

We do not offer instalment payment ourselves. PayPal may offer you a pay-in-instalments option; in that case your contractual partner is PayPal alone.

Vouchers

Vouchers can be redeemed during checkout. One voucher may be redeemed per order. Vouchers cannot be paid out in cash or offset against orders already completed. Promotional vouchers cannot be combined with other discount campaigns.

Refunds

Refunds are made by the same means you used to pay. For advance bank transfer we refund to the account the payment came from; for credit card payments the amount is credited to the card that was charged.

Prices and currency

All prices are final prices in euro and include statutory value added tax. The shipping costs stated under Shipping and Delivery apply in addition to the prices shown.
